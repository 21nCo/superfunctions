TypeScript rejects nonfinite/fractional values. Options/field selections and image nested transforms/security policy are copied before asynchronous work. Progress totals and result positions refer to the admitted snapshot. Public-only router destination policy is unchanged. Python option-validation errors map to sanitized INVALID_ARGUMENT/HTTP400 rather than leaking Pydantic input values through HTTP500.
